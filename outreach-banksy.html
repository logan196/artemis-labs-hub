Grohar Lab: Patrick J. Grohar

University of Michigan

Where EWS::FLI1 activity goes after trabectedin. The EWS::FLI1 target-high tumour domain nearly disappears after one trabectedin infusion in 4 of 5 patients, and a p53 response shows up in every patient.

Dataset: Ten paired biopsies from five patients, before and after one trabectedin infusion (GEO GSE322640).

Basal cell carcinoma · GEO GSE320484

Wong Lab: Sunny Y. Wong, PhD, Kirk D. Wuepper Professor of Molecular Genetic Dermatology

Dermatology University of Michigan

Where Hedgehog-high tumour cells sit, and what imiquimod changes. Hh-high, Mycn-high Tumor 1-like cells form a rim one to two cells deep against the stroma; imiquimod shrinks it in both mice.

Lymphangioleiomyomatosis model · GEO GSE288663

Bulun Lab: Serdar E. Bulun

Northwestern University

Does RU486 prevent the fibrosis E2+P4 causes in abdominal muscle?. E2+P4 doubles Lum+/Fbn1+ fibroblasts and cuts myofibres; RU486 prevents both, in both mice.

Dataset: Six Xenium lower abdominal muscle sections: vehicle, E2+P4, E2+P4+RU486 (GEO GSE288663).

HNSCC xenografts · GEO GSE302128

Sandulache Lab: Vlad C. Sandulache

Baylor College of Medicine

Does gedatolisib lower tumour hypoxia, or just leave smaller tumours?. At the same distance from a vessel, gedatolisib lowers the tumour hypoxia program by 36% in all four treated mice.

Dataset: Eight Xenium 5K sections, 4 saline and 4 gedatolisib (GEO GSE302128).
